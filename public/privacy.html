Privacy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Silhoueds is a daily guessing game. It collects as little as it can get away with, and sells none of it.

Playing without an account

The moment you start a puzzle the game gives your browser a signed session cookie. It holds a random identifier and nothing else — no name, no email. Against that identifier the server stores which puzzles you have played, the guesses you made, and whether you won. Progress lives on the server so the answer never has to be sent to your browser before you have earned it.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional, and the only thing it changes is that your stats follow you to another device instead of starting over there. If you do sign in, Google tells us three things, which we store: your Google account identifier, your email address, and your display name. We ask Google for nothing else — no contacts, no files, no calendar. You can play the whole game without ever signing in.

What we do not do

There are no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no tracking pixels. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold. The site is served from Fly.io, whose servers keep ordinary web logs.

Deleting your data

Signing out clears the cookie. To have an account and its play history deleted outright, email hedjohnston@gmail.com and it will be removed. You can also revoke the game's access from your Google account permissions page.
